🌠 Our Portfolio — Two Best Friends
Welcome to our little corner of the internet! 💻

 This is a personal portfolio website I created with my best friend to share our interests, achievements, favourite music and movies, and some of the projects we have worked on.

I wanted to create something personal and creative while practising the basics of web development.

 What's Inside?
The website has four main sections:

Home — a short introduction and a photo of us.
 Portfolio — our achievements in school, science, competitions, and dance.
 What We Like — our favourite playlists, singers, actors, and movies, including Spotify playlists.
 Our Projects — links to some of our previous projects and creative work.
 The footer also includes our contact information and Instagram.

 Technologies
I built this project mainly with:

 HTML5 — for the structure and content of the website.
 CSS3 — for styling, colours, gradients, cards, layouts, hover effects, and animations.
 Flexbox — to organise the cards and different sections of the page.
 Spotify embeds — to make our music section more interactive.
 What I Learned
This was one of my first web development projects, so I learned a lot while building it. HTML and CSS 

I practised creating a structured webpage with HTML, styling different elements with CSS, using Flexbox, working with images and external links, embedding Spotify playlists, and creating a more interesting visual design with gradients, cards, and hover effects.

I also learned that coding involves a lot of experimenting and problem-solving. Sometimes things didn't work the way I expected, so I had to look for solutions, change my code, and try again. This helped me become more confident and comfortable with web development.

 Why I Made It
 I wanted to create something that represents me and my best friend, rather than just making a basic practice website. Our portfolio combines coding with what we enjoy—music, dance, science, movies, writing, and creative projects.

It was also a fun way to learn how an idea can turn into a real website through creativity, experimentation, and teamwork.

Thanks for checking out our project! 💜

Keep creating, keep experimenting, and have fun coding!
